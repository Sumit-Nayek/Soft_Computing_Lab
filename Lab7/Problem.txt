An energy company wants to invest in a renewable energy project such as solar, wind, hydro, or biomass. Each alternative has advantages and limitations in terms of cost, efficiency, environmental
impact, and reliability. Some criteria are measurable, while others depend on expert opinion. Uncertainty in weather patterns and future returns makes decision-making complex. Decision-makers
need a structured approach to evaluate these alternatives. MCDM techniques provide a systematic framework for such analysis. Therefore, perform a comparative study using AHP, Fuzzy AHP, TOPSIS,
and Fuzzy TOPSIS to identify the best renewable energy source.
